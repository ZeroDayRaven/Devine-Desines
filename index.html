Why Hand-Coded Websites Are Better for SEO

18 July 2025 · 5 min read

In today's digital landscape, the foundation of your website matters more than ever. Page builders like Wix or Elementor often generate bloated, messy code that slows down your site and confuses search engines.

Hand-coded websites, on the other hand, are built with clean, semantic HTML and CSS. This allows Google's crawlers to easily understand your content structure, which directly improves your rankings.

Additionally, hand-coded sites are significantly faster – a key ranking factor. At Devine Designs, we craft every site from scratch, ensuring you get the speed and SEO advantage your business deserves.

5 Signs Your Website Needs Mobile Optimization

15 July 2025 · 4 min read

  • High bounce rate – if visitors leave within seconds, your mobile experience is likely poor.
  • Slow loading – mobile users expect pages to load in under 2 seconds.
  • Unreadable text – if users have to zoom to read, you're losing them.
  • Broken layouts – elements overlapping or cut off signal a lack of responsive design.
  • Low conversion – if your forms or buttons are hard to tap on mobile, sales will drop.

We fix all of these by building mobile-first, ensuring your site looks and performs perfectly on every device.
